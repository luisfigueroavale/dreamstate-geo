# Paid search

Use `visibility.research.web_search` only for a gap the owner named, or a group that has impressions and no page. The plan cost is 2 credits a search.

Quote first, wait for the owner's yes. Show the quote, then wait. Count the search against the weekly cap in `search.local/plan.md`. If the cap is blank, stop and ask for a number. Do not assume a cap of zero, and do not assume the cap is unlimited.
