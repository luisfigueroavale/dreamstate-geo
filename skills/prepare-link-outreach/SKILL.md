# Prepare link outreach

Draft link pitches. Do not send them. Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when

- The week plan includes one outreach batch.
- The owner wants pitches for pages that already cite the topic.

## Steps

1. Read visibility.citations and pick pages that rank or that AI answers cite (`references/pick.md`).

2. For the target pages, batch up to 5 URLs and quote visibility.research.urls_fetch at 2 credits a call, quote first, wait for the owner's yes, before the fetch (`references/fetch.md`).

3. Check the public contact path on that page, and skip the target when none is public (`references/contact.md`).

4. Draft one pitch and one follow-up, and keep them as drafts (`references/draft.md`).

5. Do not send, and hand the drafts to the owner (`references/handback.md`).

## Capabilities

- `visibility.citations`
- `visibility.research.urls_fetch`

## Stop

- Quote first, wait for the owner's yes, before each fetch.
- Do not send the pitch.
- Do not guess an email address that is not on the page.
- Skip anyone on the leave-alone list.
