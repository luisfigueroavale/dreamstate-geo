# Plan the search week

Turn the latest day report into at most four packets. Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when

- The day report is fresh and the owner wants a week plan.
- You are choosing the next pages, not writing them.

## Steps

1. Read the latest file in work/day/ and search.local/plan.md (`references/read.md`).

2. Pick at most four pieces: refreshes first, then one new page, one health fix, and one outreach batch (`references/pick.md`).

3. Write a Chief of Staff packet for each piece with Goal, Inputs, Skill, and Guardrails (`references/packet.md`).

4. Leave every paid step as a quote request inside the packet, and do not run it (`references/packet.md`).

5. Hand the packets back and do not open a pull request in this skill (`references/handback.md`).

## Capabilities

- None. This skill reads local files only.

## Stop

- Do not exceed four pieces.
- Do not put a leave-alone query into the plan.
- Do not spend, send, or merge.
- A packet is not approval to ship.
