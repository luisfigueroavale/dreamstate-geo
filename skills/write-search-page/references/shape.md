# Shape

Frontmatter needs `title`, `description`, `keyword`, `conversion`, and `page_type`. Title is 60 characters or fewer. Description is 120 to 158 characters. The keyword appears in the title, the H1, and the opening paragraph.

The opening, before the first `##`, is the answer, and it is 60 words or fewer. Under each `##`, the first sentence answers the heading in 25 words or fewer.

Add at least two internal links and one link to the conversion path. Comparison pages include `named_product` and `named_product_url`, and the URL appears in the body. Images have alt text. If you add an FAQ, the visible questions and the FAQ markup match.
