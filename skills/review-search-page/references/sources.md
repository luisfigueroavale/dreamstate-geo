# Re-read a source

Use the brief's source blocks first. Fetch again with `visibility.research.urls_fetch` only when the date is old enough that the owner doubts it, or the draft says something the brief does not contain.

The cost is 2 credits a call, for 1 to 5 URLs. Quote first, wait for the owner's yes. If the owner says no, score the unsourced line as a failed gate and do not fetch.
