# Record

Append a row to `search.local/changes.json` with `example` set to false, a new id, the page, the page type, kind `refresh`, `shipped_on` null, and `first_crawled_on` null.

After the owner says the merge happened, and a later sitemap or crawl date is known, fill `shipped_on` and `first_crawled_on`. Do not invent the crawl date.
