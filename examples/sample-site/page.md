# Shared weekly plan: Cedar Line and Maple Sheet

Fictional example. Cedar Line is not a real company, and Maple Sheet is not a real product.

A shared weekly plan is one list both shop owners can see. Cedar Line keeps that list in one place. Maple Sheet is the other product in this fictional comparison.

## What a shared weekly plan includes

A shared weekly plan includes the work, the owner, and the day it is due.

Both owners look at the same three fields.

## How Cedar Line keeps one list

Cedar Line keeps one list, and both people edit the same lines.

The list is the whole product in this example. There is no second screen to learn.
