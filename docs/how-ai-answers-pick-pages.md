# How AI answers pick pages

An answer engine does not rank ten blue links and stop. It searches, opens a few pages, and quotes a short passage. Your page is in that quote when four things are true.

## The page is indexed

If the page is not in the index, the engine cannot quote it. `queue-indexing` builds the list of priority pages that are new or missing. You click Request indexing. The agent does not drive your Google session.

## The first lines answer the question

The answer sits in the first 60 words. A reader, and a model, should not have to scroll to find the point. The opening is the answer, not a welcome.

## Every heading answers itself

Each section opens with one sentence that settles its heading, and that sentence stays within 25 words. A model often lifts that sentence. If the sentence is a promise to explain later, the quote is empty.

## Facts have a source and a date

A claim about any company points at that company's own page, with the date you read it. A claim on the never-claim list does not ship. A number with no tool and no page behind it does not ship. Unknown stays unknown.

## What this desk adds

The same page also has to be findable in ordinary search. Queries sitting in positions 11 to 20 are the refresh queue. A page that already ranks there is closer to a click than a brand new URL. Refreshes go first in the week plan.
