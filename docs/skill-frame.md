# Skill frame

Every skill under `skills/` follows this file. The skill itself stays short. Do not paste this page back into the skill.

## Read

Before a draft, read `brand/AGENTS.md`, then the filled files in `brand.local/`. `Status: blank`, or a missing file, means stop and ask. Do not guess a voice line or a claim.

Before you write for a reader, read `audience/AGENTS.md`, then `audience.local/ideal-reader.md`. The reader is that file. The buyer is the Ideal Customer page in Company Context. Do not merge them. Do not treat the template questions as the owner's answers.

Before you pick a topic or a page, read `search.local/plan.md`. If it is missing or still the blank template, stop. The leave-alone list in that file is a stop, not a suggestion.

## Consent

Nothing publishes, merges, posts, sends, drives a Google session, or spends without the owner's yes on that exact item. Fetch every tool with `tools_get` before `tools_run`. The fetched schema wins over this repo.

A paid call says: quote first, wait for the owner's yes. Show the quote, then wait. A refused quote is a price, not a yes. A free read that does not support a dry run can run live. Say that it was a read.

## Facts

Do not invent a metric, a customer, or a product fact. Dreamstate plus the local files are the source. Ids and costs are in `docs/tool-map.md`. Those ids were not confirmed with `tools_get` when this repo was written. If a fetch says not found, stop.

Only figures a tool returned go into a report. Unknown is not zero.

Every outside fact names that company's own page and the date it was read. A claim on the never-claim list does not ship.

## Writing

Drafted pages use no em dash and no en dash. Use a period, a comma, or and.

Banned words are the encoded list in `scripts/banned-words.json`. The check and the page linter decode that list. A writing skill does not copy the list into the page. Words the owner adds under Banned words in `brand.local/voice.md` add to the list for that run. They do not replace it.

Every draft also passes `docs/ai-tells.md` before the owner sees it. That list adds to the banned words. It does not replace them. A habit the owner's own posts repeat, cited by example, can stay, except the long dash, which never stays.

## Ship

The writer does not review their own page. The reviewer does not rewrite it in the same pass. The page leaves as a pull request. Merge waits for a yes.
