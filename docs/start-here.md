# Start here

This repo is a folder of skills. An agent uses the skills to get a site found in Google and named in AI answers. You approve every page that goes live and every message that goes out.

GEO means generative engine optimization. It is SEO for AI answers. An answer engine searches the web, then quotes a short passage from a page. The pages it quotes are indexed, answer the question in the first lines, answer again under every heading, and back each fact with a source.

If you are new, do the first 30 minutes in the [README](../README.md), then come back to this page.

## What you fill in

The files in `brand/`, `audience/`, `search/`, and `briefs/` are blank. Copy them to `brand.local/`, `audience.local/`, and `search.local/` and fill those copies. Git ignores the local folders.

Company facts stay in Company Context. `search.local/plan.md` points at those pages. It does not paste them.

## What the agent will not do

It will not publish a page, merge, post, send, request anything in your Google account, or spend credits without your yes on that item. It will not invent a fact or a number.
